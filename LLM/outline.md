# Defence outline: ten minutes

Every figure is pending a model run. Do not present a number until its run is recorded in `runs/`.

1. **The task and the users (1 min).** What the component does for Albert's Marketplace, and the kind of component (classifier).
2. **How it works (2 min).** One diagram: ticket, prompt, model, schema check, re-prompt (at most twice), output check, what is sent.
3. **How well, at what cost (3 min).** The strategy table [pending run], the Pareto front [pending run], cost per 1,000 calls [pending run], and what 30 cases can and cannot tell.
4. **How it fails, and how it resists misuse (2 min).** One failure spelled out: input, output, expected, why [pending run]. The injection tests [pending live run; code defences are tested offline].
5. **The recommendation (1 min).** What we would deploy, and what we would do next.

Keep one minute spare. Rehearse once, timed, with every member speaking.
